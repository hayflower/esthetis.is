01  WHO
------------------------------------------------------------------------

I'm an independent infrastructure consultant. I build and review the
systems small teams run on, along with the deployment and security work
that keeps them running.

Usually someone wants to run their own thing rather than rent it. Their
own servers, their own keys. I help them get there, then get out of the
way.

Most of the people who hire me are small: a founder, or a team of two or
three with nobody to spare for this. Often they could do it themselves
given a spare month.

A good share of the work has been on decentralized systems, where node
operation and key management make ordinary infrastructure mistakes
expensive. That's a specialty rather than the whole practice, and it has
its own entry below.

There's one person here. You talk to me rather than an account manager,
and you don't get handed off to a team once the contract is signed. You
get a written scope up front and work you can read line by line.

02  WHAT
------------------------------------------------------------------------

infrastructure/
  Servers, networks and the deployment around them. Self-hosted where
  that makes sense, and boring wherever it can be. I'll push you away
  from dependencies you can't leave, including me.

  covers:
    Linux hosts and containers. Deployment and configuration that can be
    rebuilt from the repository. Monitoring, alerting and backups that
    have actually been restored. Private networking and tunnelled access
    in place of open ports. Getting off hosted providers you would
    rather not depend on.

  you get:
    Running infrastructure with runbooks for operating it, plus an
    architecture document you can hand to whoever comes after me.

security/
  I read your code and your infrastructure and tell you what's wrong
  with it. Threat model first, so the review is about your actual
  adversary and not a checklist.

  covers:
    Web application review. Deployment and infrastructure configuration.
    Dependencies and supply chain. Key and secret handling. Operational
    security for teams too small to have anyone assigned to it.

  you get:
    A written report with reproduction steps and fixes, ranked by what
    an attacker would reach for first. Not a severity spreadsheet. One
    re-check after you've fixed things is part of the job, not a second
    engagement.

design/
  Interfaces, mostly for things that sit on top of a node or an API,
  which is where most web front-ends come apart. I don't have a
  framework to defend, and if it can be a static page it stays a static
  page.

  covers:
    Dashboards over operational or indexer data. Request and transaction
    states, including every way they fail. Key handling in the browser.
    Design systems and accessibility. Getting it to load fast on a bad
    connection. Wallet connection flows, usually Ethereum or Solana.

  you get:
    The running front-end and its source, with written notes on why it's
    built the way it is.

chains/
  The specialty. A blockchain node is infrastructure with less room for
  error, since a node that falls out of sync or a key handled carelessly
  costs more than downtime. I'll push you toward self-custody and away
  from anything that needs a trusted middleman.

  covers:
    Node deployment and monitoring. RPC and indexing strategy. Contract
    integration and ABI handling. Signing and key management. Most of
    that has been Ethereum and Solana, with Monero where the privacy
    properties are the point, though the node and key problems look much
    the same wherever you are.

  you get:
    Nodes and indexers run under the same runbooks and monitoring as the
    rest of your infrastructure, and integration code you can read line
    by line.

03  PROCESS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  01  Mail
      Tell me what you're building and what's worrying you. A paragraph
      is enough to start.

  02  Call
      Thirty minutes, no charge. We work out whether I'm the right
      person for it. Sometimes I'm not, and I'll tell you so and point
      you at someone better.

  03  Scope
      I write down what I'll do and what it costs, with a date attached.
      You approve that before any work starts, and if the scope changes
      later it changes in writing.

  04  Work
      Progress arrives in writing as it happens. No standing status
      meetings unless you want them.

  05  Handover
      Everything documented, in files you keep. I stay reachable
      afterwards for questions about what I built.

04  TERMS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  rates       On request. You get a fixed price with the written scope,
              before anything starts.
  shape       Fixed scope and fixed price where the work can honestly be

              weren't in it.

05  RECORDS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The site collects nothing about you, and I keep as little as the work
allows. Here is what that means in practice.

  collected   Whatever you choose to put in a message, and nothing
              beyond it. There is no intake form and no questionnaire.
  credentials Scoped and short-lived, or read-only where read-only is
              enough. I won't ask for a password that outlives the work.
  retained    Correspondence for as long as the engagement needs it.
              Your code and your credentials, never past handover.
  handover    Findings and artifacts go to a repository you control.
              Whatever sat on my side is destroyed, and I tell you when
              that is done.
