Privacy

What this site collects, and what sees you on the way here.

Effective 2026-08-24 · Revised 2026-11-01

In your browser

Nothing is collected. No cookies are set and no analytics run. Every file this page loads comes from this domain, the fonts included, so your browser is never told to go and fetch something from somewhere else.

You can confirm that in your network tab, which is a stronger guarantee than this paragraph is. The page also works with JavaScript switched off.

How this reaches you

This site sits behind a Cloudflare tunnel. Your request arrives at a Cloudflare edge server, which passes it down an outbound connection to the machine holding the files. The machine's address is published nowhere, which is the reason for the arrangement.

You should know what that costs you. Cloudflare terminates the encryption, so they see your IP address and every page you ask for, with timestamps. They can read the traffic in both directions. That is true of every site behind a CDN, and it is worth stating plainly rather than leaving you to find it with dig.

What Cloudflare does with that is governed by them and not by me. Their policy is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

If you would rather not have Cloudflare in the path, reach me on Signal at @esth.01. That route does not touch this site or its mail.

On my server

Because the tunnel sits in front, my server never learns your IP address. The connection it sees comes from Cloudflare, and the only place your real address appears is in a header I have chosen not to record.

Access logging on my side is switched off. There is no log on my machine that could identify you, truncated or otherwise, so there is nothing on my side to leak. Cloudflare keeps their own logs on their own terms.

Do Not Track

Some browsers send a Do Not Track header. This site does not act on it, because there is nothing here for it to switch off. No tracking happens whether the header arrives or not.

Nothing on this site follows you to another one. There are no embeds and no advertising code of any kind. Cloudflare's view of you is limited to the requests you make to this domain.

If you mail me

When you email me I have your address and whatever you chose to put in the message. That is ordinary, but it is worth being precise about where it goes.

Mail to this domain is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing and delivered to a mailbox elsewhere. Either can read anything that arrives unencrypted, exactly as with any hosted mail.

That is the same company that fronts this site, so Cloudflare is in a position to see both your visit and your message. I would rather write that down than let you assume the two paths are separate.

I keep correspondence for as long as an engagement needs it and a while afterwards for my own records. Ask me to delete yours and I will.

Your choices

Mail me and ask what I hold about you. Ask me to correct it or delete it and I will do that, and I will tell you when it is done.

The CCPA sets revenue and volume thresholds that a practice this size does not come close to, so it does not formally apply to me. I intend to honour requests as though it did. Nothing here is sold or shared for advertising, which is the thing that statute mostly exists to govern.

If you are in the UK or the EU and the GDPR applies to your dealings with me, the same applies. Ask, and you get an answer rather than a form.

Changes

The revision date at the top of this page changes whenever the text does. If something material changes I will say what changed rather than quietly swapping the wording.
